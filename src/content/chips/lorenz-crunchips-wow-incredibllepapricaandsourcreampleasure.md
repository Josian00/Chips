A refreshing combination of classic paprika with sour cream. The sweetness of the paprika blends nicely with the only hinted-at tang of the sour cream. The creaminess that comes along with it rounds the whole thing off perfectly. The taste makes you want to reach for another one.

These chips combine the classic Crunchips Paprika we've known for a long time with sour cream and a crinkle cut, which gives the product a premium feel. So if you're a fan of the classic and like to try something new, these chips are a good fit for you.

From my own experience, though, I have to warn anyone who tends to eat a whole bag in one go, as this can lead to slight discomfort due to the high fat content. For pros, however, this shouldn't be a problem.
